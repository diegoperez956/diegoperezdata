# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

AI engineer / ML engineer / quant recruiters and hiring managers, skimming on desktop between candidate reviews. Secondary: engineers who got the link from a resume or GitHub and are deciding in under a minute whether Diego is real. Job to be done: verify he ships real AI/ML work and has taste, fast.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Diego Perez (diegoperezdata.com), styled as an Apache mod_autoindex directory listing. The metaphor is the point: a portfolio that reads like a filesystem, not a brochure. Success: a recruiter opens 2 things (a repo, the resume) and comes away thinking "senior, sharp, a little weird in a good way."

## Brand Personality

Terminal-native, dry-witted, precise. Old-internet affection without nostalgia cosplay. The site is itself a demo: loads instantly, no framework, gets out of the way. Emotional goal: quiet confidence, the kind that doesn't need a hero section.

## Anti-references

- Brochure portfolios: hero banner, headshot carousel, "Hi, I'm Diego 👋" template.
- SaaS landing aesthetics: gradient text, glass cards, identical card grids.
- Sanitized corporate blandness. The Terry Davis quote, the mkdir gag, and the political poster art stay.
- Anything that needs a spinner.

## Design Principles

- The index is the work: every element behaves like a filesystem artifact (rows, dates, types, paths).
- Fast is the brand: no framework, no dependency, nothing blocking first paint.
- Deadpan commitment: gags are executed precisely, never winked at.
- Content over chrome: the pane shows the work; navigation never competes with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard: links activate with Enter; buttons activate with Enter or Space. Visible focus rings (orange outline). Honor prefers-reduced-motion for any added animation. Contrast: gruvbox palette on #282828, keep text ≥ 4.5:1.
